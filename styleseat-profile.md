### Service descriptions (paste one per service)

**In-Salon Consultation**
> 30 minutes to look at your hair and scalp, talk through your goals and choose the right method, size and price. Best for first-time loc clients, anyone transferring from another stylist, and locs that are overdue, thinning or matted. You leave with a plan and a quote.

**Loc Retwist (Palm Roll)**
> Clean-parted palm roll retwist with a shampoo included. Done with low tension at the base so your roots stay full. Best every 4 to 6 weeks. Overdue, matted or thinning locs? Book the consultation first.

**Partial Retwist**
> Retwist of the perimeter and the parts that show: hairline, edges and top. A good in-between or pre-event refresh. Not a replacement for full maintenance.

**Starter Locs**
> Start your loc journey with a loctician who plans for loc health. Choose small, medium or large. Price starts at $140 and depends on size, density and length, confirmed at consultation. Plan 3 to 5 hours and eat before you come.

**Instant Locs**
> Mature-looking locs in one appointment, installed with the crochet method. Partial installs take about 3 hours, full heads 4 to 6 hours.

**Interlocking Loc Maintenance**
> Interlocking retwist for a longer hold between visits, ideal for active clients and fine hair. Best every 6 to 10 weeks. Full head.

**Microloc Retie**
> Micro loc maintenance priced by how many weeks it has been since your last visit. Pick the option closest to your timeline.

**Microloc Extensions**
> Small, lightweight locs with extensions. Final price depends on loc count, length and install time, confirmed at consultation.

**Two-Strand Twists**
> Neat two-strand twists on natural hair, or with hair added for length. A protective style that gives your hair a break. Wash and prep included.

**Goddess Locs**
> Soft, bohemian goddess locs installed with care for your edges. Choose your length when booking.

**Silk Press**
> A smooth, healthy silk press that still feels like your hair on wash day. Prep, heat and finish planned around your hair's condition.
